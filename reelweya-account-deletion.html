ReelWeya — Account and Data Deletion

This page explains how users of ReelWeya can request deletion of their account and associated personal data.

How to request account deletion

  1. Send an email to [ADD YOUR SUPPORT EMAIL] with the subject ReelWeya Account Deletion Request.
  2. Include the email address or user ID associated with your ReelWeya account so we can identify it. Do not send your password.
  3. We may contact you to verify that you own the account before processing the request.

What data is deleted

When an account-deletion request is verified and completed, ReelWeya will delete the account and the personal information associated with it, including profile information and user-generated content linked to the account, except for information that must be retained for a lawful purpose.

What data may be retained

Some records may be retained where required by law, to resolve disputes, prevent fraud or enforce applicable terms. Any retained information will be limited to what is necessary for that purpose and protected appropriately.

Deletion timeline

We aim to process verified deletion requests within 30 days. If any data must be retained, it will be kept only for the period required for the applicable purpose. Data in routine backups may remain until those backups are rotated out.
